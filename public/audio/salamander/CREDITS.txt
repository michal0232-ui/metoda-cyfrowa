Salamander Grand Piano — Alexander Holm
Instrument recorded: Yamaha C5.
Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

This application redistributes a subset of the MP3 files without editing them.
Playback transposes the nearest recording by at most one semitone for the
current exercise range, and applies gain and a short release envelope.
This is a single dynamic layer, not the full multi-velocity SFZ instrument.
The upstream README is included as SOURCE-README.txt.
